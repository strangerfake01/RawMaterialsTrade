3. Required and optional data

Fields marked with an asterisk are necessary to identify and answer the enquiry. Without them, the form cannot be submitted and we may be unable to provide a quotation or documentation. Optional fields help us prepare a more accurate response.

4. Data categories

Name, company, country, business email, optional telephone and company/tax identifier, product, quantity, industry, requested documents, message, source page, language and submission time. Cloudflare may temporarily process the IP address, browser, device and network signals and a Turnstile token for security verification. The form endpoint also uses a short-lived IP-based rate-limit key and a one-way hash of the business email address to limit repeated submissions; the raw email address is not stored in the rate-limit key.

If analytics is accepted, Google Analytics may process the page path without query parameters, page title, referrer without query parameters, approximate location, language, browser and device information, randomly generated cookie identifiers, page views, scrolls, outbound clicks, file downloads and the successful submission event of the enquiry form. Form field contents, names, email addresses, telephone numbers and message text are not sent to Google Analytics. For users in the EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, Google states that individual IP addresses are not logged or stored by Google Analytics; IP data is used to derive coarse location and then discarded.

5. Recipients and processors

Access is limited to authorised Raw Materials Trade staff. The static website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Cloudflare provides Turnstile and the protected form endpoint. Google Apps Script and Google Workspace transmit and store accepted enquiries in the company mailbox. If analytics is accepted, Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, provides Google Analytics as a data processor under its applicable data-processing terms. We do not sell personal data.

6. International transfers

GitHub, Cloudflare and Google may process data using infrastructure outside the EEA. Where required, transfers rely on an adequacy decision, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for participating entities, or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses together with supplementary safeguards.

7. Retention

  • Unsuccessful or closed enquiries: up to 12 months from closure.
  • Quotation and negotiation correspondence: up to 24 months from closure, unless a contract, dispute or legal claim requires longer retention.
  • Contractual, accounting and tax records: for the period required by applicable law.
  • Worker rate-limit keys derived from the IP address and a one-way email hash: up to 10 minutes in the configured Cloudflare KV store.
  • The analytics consent choice remains in local storage until it is changed or the browser storage is cleared. Google Analytics cookies are configured for a maximum of approximately 13 months. User-level and event-level Analytics data is retained according to the property setting, for no more than 14 months in a standard GA4 property.
  • Provider security and diagnostic logs: only for the period configured in the relevant Cloudflare, GitHub and Google services and as necessary for fraud prevention, troubleshooting and security.

8. Security and anti-abuse controls

The form uses validation, a honeypot, completion-time checks, field and payload limits, rate limiting and mandatory server-side Cloudflare Turnstile verification. No security measure can guarantee absolute security, but access and processing are limited to what is necessary.
