Where this company fits in B2B procurement

Useful for procurement teams that need

A B2B chemical raw-material supplier, sourcing partner or availability and documentation support in Hungary, Austria, DACH, Central Europe or wider Europe.

Useful for manufacturers that need

A Hungarian or Central European sales, representation, distribution or supplier-network partner for chemical raw materials.

Relevant product and sourcing fields

Industrial chemicals, food and feed raw materials, cosmetic and personal care ingredients, water treatment chemicals, home care raw materials, de-icing materials and specialty chemicals.

Do not confuse with

A consumer retail shop, general webshop or safety-documentation authority. Final suitability, classification and handling always require official SDS/TDS/CoA and supplier documentation.
