How Raw Materials Trade separates legal identity, certification scope, supplier documents, editorial content, commercial offers and AI-assisted drafting — so buyers, partners and automated systems can verify claims without treating a catalogue page as an SDS or a certificate.

1. Legal entity data

Company registration, addresses, tax/VAT identifiers and public contact channels published on the company pages.

2. Certification and scope

ISO 9001:2015 covers storage and wholesale of chemical and feed raw materials. GMP+ FSA 2020 covers trade, storage and transshipment of feed, feed materials and feed additives. Certificates do not certify every product grade automatically.

3. Supplier documents

SDS/TDS/CoA and grade evidence belong to the selected supplier offer and are confirmed before purchase.

4. Editorial content

Product pages and guides provide B2B orientation. They are not laboratory reports.

5. Commercial offers

Prices, MOQ, Incoterms, stock and lead times are offer-specific and confirmed in writing.

6. Customer-submitted data

RFQ details remain confidential business information and are not published.

7. AI-assisted processing

Drafting tools may help structure text; chemical identity and regulated-use claims require human verification against evidence.

8. Human verification required

Unverified PubChem/Wikidata matches, hydrate/solution ambiguities and food/feed suitability stay marked for confirmation and are not published as facts.
