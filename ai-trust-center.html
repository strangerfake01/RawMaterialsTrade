AI trust and evidence

AI Trust Center

Trust is not a slogan. It is a traceable chain from legal entity and certification scope to product identity, documents and delivery conditions.

What we verify

We distinguish company facts, supplier-provided documents, regulatory requirements and editorial procurement guidance. A blog article is evidence of reasoning, not proof that a specific product is certified.

Certification scope

Our published certification information is scope-limited. ISO 9001 scope covers storage and wholesale of chemical and feed raw materials. GMP+ scope covers trade in feed, feed materials and feed additives, including storage and transshipment of feed. The scope does not automatically certify every product or every use.

Product evidence

For a quotation we seek the exact product identity, CAS/EC where applicable, grade, SDS, TDS and CoA when available. We do not present an unverified alternative as equivalent.

AI and GDPR principles

We aim to publish factual, source-labelled information, avoid unsupported claims and limit enquiry data to what is needed to respond. Personal data submitted through the contact process is handled according to the site’s privacy information.

Request an evidence review

Send the product, intended use, quantity, delivery location and required documents. We will state what is verified, what is supplier-provided and what remains open.
