Professional profile

Experience built across the chemical raw material value chain

Alexandra combines operational distribution experience with strategic procurement, international sales and company leadership. Her work focuses on connecting documented raw material supply with the real production, purchasing and logistics needs of industrial partners.

Career and international experience

Alexandra began her career at Brenntag Hungária, where she learned chemical distribution from operational foundations through procurement, sales, logistics and multinational processes. At Variachem Kft. she worked as a product manager with industrial, feed, food, cosmetic and home-care raw materials.

Through Variachem’s WACKER representation she participated in professional training covering cosmetic, home-care and silicone raw materials. Her feed-market work led to Solvay representation responsibilities. She has worked with international companies including Solvay, BASF, Dow and WACKER.

Working method

She evaluates enquiries in the context of target grade, production schedule, purchasing cycle, documentation, logistics and market conditions—not only by product name. Final suitability and availability are confirmed through current supplier SDS/TDS/CoA and commercial documentation.

Business principles

Trust, transparency, discretion, direct communication and sustainable win–win cooperation guide her partner relationships.
