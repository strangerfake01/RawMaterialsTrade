How an initial evaluation works

Industrial buyers can use Raw Materials Trade Kft. for quotation-based sourcing, alternative supplier searches, product availability checks and documentation requests. This two-sided market understanding helps manufacturers reach relevant demand rather than undifferentiated traffic.

1. Product fitWe review product identity, quality level, application fields and documentation.
2. Market fitWe identify relevant industries, buyer profiles and geographic scope.
3. Commercial fitWe discuss availability, packaging, logistics, pricing framework and cooperation model.
4. Controlled market developmentOnly technically and commercially aligned opportunities are taken forward.

What to send for a first manufacturer / supplier discussion

  1. Company profile and role (producer, converter, trader).
  2. Product portfolio with grades and typical packaging.
  3. Target markets and any existing EU/DACH/Hungary coverage.
  4. Available quality documents (SDS/TDS/CoA samples as appropriate).
  5. Indicative capacity or order structure — without confidential customer data.
  6. Preferred cooperation model (representation, distribution, spot/recurring supply).
  7. Primary contact for commercial follow-up.

Submission does not create exclusivity, guaranteed sales or market-entry results. Qualification is case-by-case.
