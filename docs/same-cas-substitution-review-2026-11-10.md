## Mandatory review before publishing any substitution

1. Define the exact end use, process conditions and the original product specification.
2. Compare supplier-specific TDS, current SDS and representative CoA; verify chemical identity, concentration/assay, impurity limits, hydration and physical form.
3. Check EU and destination-market restrictions for the stated use, especially food, feed and pharmaceutical grades.
4. Document technical conversion factors, process validation and any required customer approval.
5. Record evidence references, verification dates, a qualified reviewer, limitations and the decision in `product-substitutions.json` using the governed schema.
6. Publish an application-specific recommendation only after the decision is `approved`; do not infer equivalence from CAS alone.
