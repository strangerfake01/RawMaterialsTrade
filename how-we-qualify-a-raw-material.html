How we qualify a raw material

A chemical name and a CAS number identify a substance. They do not, by themselves, prove that a specific grade will perform reliably in your process.

Identity first

We confirm the product name, CAS or EC number, formula, hydrate form and composition. If the identity is composition-dependent, we do not guess: we request the current SDS or TDS.

Grade and intended use

Purity, concentration, physical form and intended application determine whether a material is suitable. Food, feed, industrial and technical grades must not be treated as interchangeable.

Documentation

Before an offer is confirmed, we request the relevant supplier SDS, TDS and, where available, CoA. Regulatory suitability and certification scope are checked for the exact product, supplier and intended use.

Physical performance

Particle size, moisture, bulk density, flowability, dissolution, crystal form and insoluble impurities can affect handling and process performance even when the chemical identity is the same.

Supplier continuity

A qualified source must be able to deliver consistent quality, documentation and logistics over time. We help buyers compare alternatives without presenting an unverified source as equivalent.

What to send us

Product or CAS/EC if known, application, target grade or reference product, quantity and unit, packaging, delivery location, timing and required documents. If the grade is uncertain, describe the application.
