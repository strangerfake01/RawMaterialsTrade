Who We Are

A trusted B2B raw material sourcing partner

Raw Materials Trade Kft. is a Hungarian B2B raw material trading company built on extensive professional experience in international sourcing, procurement and partner development. We connect European industrial buyers with reliable suppliers across chemical, feed, food and specialty sectors.

Our team brings deep expertise in procurement, sales and supplier relations. We work through an established international manufacturer and distributor network, giving B2B clients flexible sourcing, quick decision-making and direct, transparent communication.

Supplier names, factory details and commercially sensitive partner information are not published. Availability, grade, documentation and logistics are confirmed individually before every B2B transaction.

Executive profile

Alexandra began her career at Brenntag Hungária, learning chemical distribution from its operational foundations and progressing through the organisation. This gave her a detailed understanding of procurement, sales, logistics and multinational business processes.

At Variachem Kft. she worked as a product manager across industrial, feed, food, cosmetic and home-care raw materials. The company’s owner and managing director remains an important professional mentor whose expertise, integrity and partner-focused approach strongly influenced Alexandra’s leadership philosophy.

Through Variachem’s role as the official Hungarian representative of WACKER, she completed professional training in cosmetic, home-care and silicone raw materials. In the feed market, her work earned the Solvay representation—one of the defining professional recognitions of her career.

Today, as owner and managing director of Raw Materials Trade Kft., she is responsible for strategic leadership, international procurement, business development and the creation of sustainable long-term partnerships.

International experience

Alexandra has worked with major international companies including Solvay, BASF, Dow and WACKER. Her active supplier and customer relationships extend across Europe, North America, Asia and the Middle East, including Germany, France, Italy, Austria, the United States, China and Israel.

Working method

She aims to understand each partner’s production schedule, purchasing cycle and operational priorities—not simply the requested material. By monitoring market prices, energy and freight costs, and supply-chain developments, she supports timely and well-founded procurement decisions.

Business principles

Her approach is built on trust, transparency, discretion and win–win cooperation. She communicates directly and addresses difficult questions early, helping partners avoid delays, misunderstandings and unnecessary cost.
