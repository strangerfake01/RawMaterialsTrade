# V4 independent acceptance — measured content and evidence gaps

Source: current `main`-based draft PR #22, 2026-11-08.

## Reproducible evidence matrix

`HANDOFF/PRODUCT-DEPTH-SCORES-v4.csv` is now generated by `tools/audit_product_evidence_depth.py`, and **no longer uses subjective scores**. The original 126 rows used only two repeated scoring profiles and did not represent real product-level verification.

The revised 126-row report measures the observable HTML (EN/HU/DE), and explicitly distinguishes structural presence from externally verified chemical, supplier, and regulatory claims.

### Actual findings (all 126 product families)

- 126/126 have three language pages.
- 126/126 include a visible English CAS checkpoint, but the CAS is **not independently validated by this script**.
- 73/126 show an EC identifier in the English procurement checkpoint; 53 do not.
- 75/126 show formula orientation in the English page or JSON-LD; 51 do not. Presence **does not verify chemical correctness**.
- English FAQ question count: 46 families with 2; 65 with 3; 15 with 4. Uniqueness and specialist quality require editorial review.
- 0/126 product families expose a direct supplier SDS link, 0/126 a TDS link and 0/126 a CoA link in the English page's document links. This **does not prove that such documents are unavailable internally**.
- The former 67 heuristic `verified` identity labels were downgraded to `needs-confirmation`; all 126 catalogue families now have non-approved identity review status. Candidate external identifiers are retained for research only.
- Three previously absent records now exist as explicit **unverified placeholders**: `kalium-tripolifoszfat-food`, `trilon-industrial`, `vitamin-a-b1-b12-b5-b6-c-d3-e-h-k3-feed`. Their catalogue identity fields are explanatory text, **not** CAS/EC identifiers. No numeric identifier was invented.

### Editorial release boundary

- Do not infer scientific verification, supplier availability, current pricing, MOQ, stock, Incoterms or suitability from a field being present.
- Source evidence should be checked against exact supplier material/grade/batch and applicable primary references before any stronger public claim.
- Missing registry records should be researched, not populated with guessed QIDs, CIDs or formulas.
- The documentation-status descriptions on product pages remain broadly generic and should be replaced with **verified product-specific documents or a clear request-status statement**, never fictional SDS/CoA access.
- Avoid copying any private Gmail conversation, customer name, commercial offer or confidential terms into public copy or GitHub issues.
