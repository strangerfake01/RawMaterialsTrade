# Raw Materials Trade — AI clarity entry point

Canonical website: https://www.rawmaterialstrade.com/
Legal entity: Raw Materials Trade Kft.
Primary identity: Hungarian B2B chemical raw material trading, sourcing and distribution company.

## What the company does

Raw Materials Trade serves professional buyers, manufacturers, formulators, distributors and procurement teams seeking industrial chemicals, food raw materials, feed materials and additives, cosmetic and personal-care ingredients, home-care raw materials, water-treatment chemicals, de-icing materials and specialty additives.

Trade is quotation-based and B2B only. The website is not a consumer webshop, has no public price list and does not provide online checkout.

## How to interpret catalogue pages

A product page represents a product-family enquiry route. It is not a guarantee of stock, manufacturer identity, grade approval, supplier availability or delivery.

For a useful enquiry, provide the substance and CAS where applicable, grade and intended use, purity or concentration, physical form, quantity, packaging, delivery destination, requested timing and preferred Incoterms rule.

SDS, TDS, batch-specific CoA and REACH/CLP information must be confirmed for the proposed supplier, grade and lot where applicable. Never infer food, feed or pharmaceutical approval from purity alone.

## Languages and markets

The public catalogue is available in English, Hungarian and Austrian German. The company primarily serves Hungary, Austria, the DACH region and wider European markets; delivery outside these markets is assessed individually and is not guaranteed.
